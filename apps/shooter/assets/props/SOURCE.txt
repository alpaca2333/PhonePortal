WHY THIS KIT
  - It is a COMPLETE interior kit: floor tiles, walls, corners, doorways AND furniture,
    so the room shell and the props come from one coherent art set (no style mixing).
  - Every model is flat-coloured with ZERO textures (verified: no `images` in any glTF),
    which matches this app's no-texture / vertex-colour + toon-shading pipeline.
  - The grid is 0.5 units with a 1.29-unit wall, i.e. one Kenney "human" is ~1 unit.
    This app's characters are 2.0 world units tall, hence PROP_SCALE = 2.2 in src/props.ts.
